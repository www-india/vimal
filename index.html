    Know Your Symptoms

    Dental Symptom Guide

    Not sure what your tooth is telling you? Select a symptom below to understand possible causes and when to visit us at ProHealth Dental Care.

    🦷
    Tooth Pain
    ⚠ May need prompt attention

    Tooth pain is one of the most common dental complaints. It can range from a mild ache to a sharp, severe pain that disrupts daily life. The type and location of pain often indicates the underlying cause.

    Common signs & causes

    • Sharp pain when biting — may indicate a cracked tooth or cavity
    • Dull, constant ache — often a sign of infection or abscess
    • Throbbing pain — may point to nerve involvement (requiring root canal)
    • Pain after eating sweets — commonly caused by tooth decay
    • Sudden onset severe pain — could indicate a dental emergency

    Possible treatments at ProHealth

    Tooth FillingRoot Canal TherapyTooth ExtractionCrown PlacementAbscess Treatment
    🚨

    If you have severe, persistent or sudden tooth pain, please call us immediately at +91 97317 31451. Do not ignore dental pain — it rarely resolves without treatment.

    Tooth Sensitivity
    ✓ Common and treatable

    Tooth sensitivity causes a sudden, sharp flash of pain in response to hot, cold, sweet, or acidic foods and drinks. It affects millions of people and is often very manageable with the right treatment.

    Common signs & causes

    • Sharp pain when drinking hot or cold beverages
    • Discomfort when eating sweet or acidic food
    • Pain while breathing cold air through the mouth
    • Sensitivity after teeth whitening or cleaning
    • Gum recession exposing the root surface

    Possible treatments at ProHealth

    Desensitizing TreatmentFluoride ApplicationBonding / SealantsGum GraftCrown if needed
    Book Consultation📞 Call Now
    🔴
    Gum Problems
    ⚠ Should not be ignored

    Healthy gums are the foundation of a healthy smile. Bleeding, swollen, or receding gums are early warning signs of gum disease (gingivitis or periodontitis) that should be addressed promptly.

    Common signs & causes

    • Gums that bleed when brushing or flossing
    • Red, swollen, or tender gums
    • Gums that are pulling away from teeth
    • Persistent bad breath alongside gum issues
    • Loose or shifting teeth in severe cases

    Possible treatments at ProHealth

    Professional CleaningDeep ScalingGum Disease TreatmentLaser Gum Therapy
    Book Consultation📞 Call Now
    💥
    Broken or Chipped Tooth
    ⚠ Prompt care recommended

    A broken, chipped, or cracked tooth can occur from injury, biting hard food, or wear over time. Even small chips should be examined to prevent further damage or infection.

    Common signs & causes

    • Visible crack, chip, or fracture on the tooth
    • Sharp or jagged tooth edge that cuts the tongue
    • Pain when chewing or biting
    • Sensitivity to temperature in the affected area
    • Old crown or filling that has broken or fallen out

    Possible treatments at ProHealth

    Dental BondingCrown / CapVeneerRoot Canal if infectedDental Scan
    Book Consultation📞 Call Now
    🕳️
    Missing Tooth
    ✓ Permanent solutions available

    A missing tooth is more than a cosmetic concern — it can cause bone loss, shifting of neighbouring teeth, and difficulty chewing. Modern implant solutions offer a permanent, natural-looking fix.

    Common concerns & effects

    • Gap in smile affecting appearance and confidence
    • Difficulty chewing certain foods
    • Adjacent teeth drifting into the empty space
    • Bone loss in the jaw over time if untreated
    • Changes in bite or facial structure

    Possible treatments at ProHealth

    Dental ImplantsImplant-Supported BridgePartial DentureFull Arch Restoration

    🪥
    Stained or Yellow Teeth
    ✓ Safe, effective whitening available

    Discolouration is one of the most common cosmetic dental concerns. Teeth can stain from tea, coffee, tobacco, medications, or simply with age. Professional whitening delivers safe, dramatic results.

    Common causes

    • Regular consumption of tea, coffee, or red wine
    • Tobacco use (smoking or chewing)
    • Certain medications such as tetracycline
    • Natural ageing process of enamel
    • Poor oral hygiene or surface staining

    Possible treatments at ProHealth

    Professional Teeth WhiteningPorcelain VeneersDental CleaningSmile Makeover
    Book Whitening Consult📞 Call Now
    😮
    Bad Breath
    ✓ Usually treatable at the source

    Persistent bad breath (halitosis) is often a sign of an underlying dental issue rather than simply a hygiene problem. Identifying the root cause is the key to lasting freshness.

    Common signs & causes

    • Unpleasant taste or odour that doesn't go away with brushing
    • Gum disease or infection as an underlying cause
    • Food trapped in cavities or between teeth
    • Dry mouth reducing natural cleansing saliva
    • Dentures or dental appliances harbouring bacteria

    Possible treatments at ProHealth

    Professional Scaling & CleaningCavity TreatmentGum Disease TreatmentOral Hygiene Guidance
    Book Consultation📞 Call Now
    😬
    Jaw Pain or Clicking
    ⚠ Often linked to teeth grinding

    Jaw pain, clicking sounds, or difficulty opening the mouth can indicate TMJ (temporomandibular joint) disorder or bruxism (teeth grinding). These are manageable conditions with the right care.

    Common signs & causes

    • Clicking or popping sounds when opening or closing the mouth
    • Jaw pain or soreness, especially in the morning
    • Headaches or ear pain alongside jaw discomfort
    • Waking with a sore jaw (sign of night-time grinding)
    • Flat, worn-down teeth from chronic grinding

    Possible treatments at ProHealth

    Night Guard / SplintBruxism TreatmentTMJ TherapyBite Correction
